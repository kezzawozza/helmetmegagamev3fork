The Bleeding Yard Ravenheart · Location 9 here

The gate grinds open at the top of the Yard. Somebody has been bleeding on the cobbles again — it has not been washed off.

Turn 12 · Morning

Ravenheart Fortress

Decree of the Tithe Hall · read aloud at the gate

The Tower sputtered twice in the night. Until the Lifeweb is fed again, every household of the Yard owes a second tithe of blood before the bell.

Those who will not give it will be taken to give it. The Warden keeps the list.

The intercom crackles somewhere above the rooftiles, then goes quiet.

Marrow Vance: Second tithe. They say that like it costs them something.

Sister Ilda Roke: It costs them the same as us. Keep your voice down, Marrow.

Marrow Vance spits into the gutter and rubs the mark on the back of her hand.

Tobin Quell: I gave on the fourth day. My arm has not closed yet. Look at it.

Tobin pushes his sleeve back. The puncture is black at the edges and smells faintly of wet iron.

Sister Ilda Roke: That is not the tithe going bad. That is the needle. Come to the Hall and I will cut it clean — 2 ⬢ for the poultice, no more.

Tobin Quell: I have 3 ⬢ and a debt to the Merchant. Take the 2.

Somebody moves four crates of salt fish from the Yard stash toward the Cistern Stair. Nobody stops them.

Halbrecht Oss: Those are mine, before anyone asks. Bought and paid — 40 ¢ at the Depot, and I have the chit.

Wren Sallow: Nobody asked, Halbrecht. That is the fourth time you have told us.

Halbrecht Oss looks at the chit anyway, as though it might have changed.

A dog starts up under the Crow's steps and will not stop.

Wren Sallow whispers to you: the Warden's list has your name on it twice — go out by the Stair, not the gate.

Warden Crake: Yard. Stand where you are. I am reading names.

Three of the Warden's men come in behind him and take the mouth of the gate.

Marrow Vance does not stand where she is.

Marrow Vance swings a rivet hammer at Warden Crake — it lands on his shoulder plate with a flat, ugly crack.

Warden Crake staggers, and the men at the gate draw.

The Yard empties toward the Cistern Stair. Somebody is screaming about the bell.

Sister Ilda Roke: Down. Get down, all of you — Tobin, the wall!

Tobin Quell: I cannot run on this arm.

Sister Ilda Roke takes him under the good arm and hauls him behind the cistern housing.

MARROW! LEAVE IT!

The gate grinds shut. Whoever is still in the Yard is still in the Yard.
